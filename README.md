# HR Analytics Projects

This repository contains my HR Analytics portfolio projects.

## 1. Employee Attrition Prediction

**Objective:** Predict which employees are likely to leave the company and identify key factors driving attrition.

**Tools Used:**
- Python
- Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Scikit-learn (Random Forest)

**Key Insights:**
- Employees working OverTime have significantly higher attrition
- Sales and Laboratory Technician roles show higher exit rates
- Younger employees and those with lower income are more likely to leave
- Low Job Satisfaction is a strong indicator of attrition

**Business Recommendations:**
- Reduce excessive overtime
- Improve career growth for high-risk roles
- Strengthen support for younger employees
- Focus on compensation and job satisfaction
